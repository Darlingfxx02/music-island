# Music Island 3.0.2

New dictation themes, clearer model selection and Windows reliability fixes.

## Dictation

- Choose **Classic**, **Waves** or **Fox**. The preview follows your theme and plays its animations.
- Waves adds soft optical blur and glow; Fox adds animated pixel ears. Choose a named color, including White, from the theme settings.
- Recording controls now match your shortcut mode: hold-to-dictate has no Stop or Cancel button, while toggle mode shows one Finish action.
- Filter models by language with checkboxes. English and Russian appear first, recommendations name their target language, and download buttons stay at the bottom right of each card.
- Applied selected Handy fixes for shortcut validation, cancellation after short recordings, missing models, empty recordings, filler cleanup and loading feedback. Updated the native transcription bridge to transcribe-cpp 0.2.4.

## Windows and music

- Keep Direct Yandex Music launch arguments in existing Desktop, Start, taskbar and autostart entries across reboots. Choosing SMTC restores normal launches; the Yandex Music executable is unchanged. Startup and reconnect repair supported entries replaced by client updates.

- Reduced the portable payload with lossless compression of native libraries and resources. CPU, Vulkan and all recognition engines remain included.
- The portable EXE is now about 73 MiB, down from 137 MiB in the earlier 3.0.2 candidate (47% smaller).
- Verified cached voice libraries by SHA-256 and repaired damaged copies automatically.
- Made settings writes atomic and prevented late startup reads from replacing newer settings, tracks or connection status.
- Cancelled pending fox video waits when settings close and recovered transitions from media errors.
- Prepared and verified self-update replacements before switching the executable.
- Removed unused upstream CLI code and plugin dependencies; unchanged native bundles no longer invalidate the host build.

- Addressed white flashes when first hovering over the island by keeping the overlay's Windows composition style stable and initializing WebView2 with a transparent background.
- Fixed layered-parent opacity initialization to prevent clicks passing through parts of the expanded island, including its right side.
- Prevented background native helpers from opening console windows.
- Centered the Yandex Music recovery notice and added a larger primary restart button.
- Improved keyboard interaction in shared menus and language filters.

Dictation visual adaptations from [No Type](https://github.com/Darlingfxx02/no-tipe) are included with the author's permission; see [third-party notices](https://github.com/redheadesign/music-island/blob/v3.0.2/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

Download `music-island.exe` or update from About. No installation is required, and your existing settings are preserved.
